🌈 Welcome to Allura!

We're a queer/trans/plural-run collective that specializes in finetuning AI models for roleplaying and creative writing. We take existing LLMs and make them better at the things we care about most.

Our mission: Creating better roleplaying AI through careful finetuning and dataset curation, built by and for our community.
